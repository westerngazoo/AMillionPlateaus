- **Status:** Accepted · **Milestone:** POC — Knowledge content · **Created:** 2026-07-24

## 1. Statement

R-0096 twinned cuatrimestres 1–3 (10 topics) and R-0097 made the pair walkable, but `Next ›` ran
out at cuatrimestre 4. This extends the parallel route to **20 topics, through cuatrimestre 6** —
and it is where the alternative formalisms stop being nicer notation and start doing real work:

- **Cálculo IV → GA.** The gradient theorem, Green's, Stokes' and the divergence theorem are
  **one theorem**, $\int_{\partial M} dS\,F = \int_M dV\,\nabla F$, with dimension and grade as
  parameters. Cauchy's integral theorem is the same statement in 2-D.
- **Electromagnetismo II → GA.** The flagship: $F = E + IB$ and **all four** Maxwell equations
  become $\nabla F = J$. Lorentz covariance becomes manifest ($F' = L F \tilde L$) and the Lorentz
  force collapses to $\dot p = qF\cdot v$.
- **Electromagnetismo I → GA**, deliberately placed *before* it: electrostatics' two laws are the
  two grades of one product, $\nabla E = \rho/\varepsilon_0$ — the static case of $\nabla F = J$, so
  the unification next cuatrimestre is a continuation rather than a magic trick.
- **EDP → GA.** $\nabla^2 = \nabla\nabla$: the Laplacian factors, which is *why* Maxwell is
  first-order and why Dirac's factorization of Klein–Gordon is bookkeeping rather than genius.
- **Física IV → GA.** A boost is a rotation in a time–space plane; rapidity adds because rotors
  multiply, so velocity addition is just $\tanh(\alpha_1+\alpha_2)$.
- **Mecánica Cuántica I → GA.** The Pauli matrices are the basis vectors of 3-D space, a spinor is
  an even-grade element (a rotor), and the 720° is because $R$ and $-R$ give the same rotation.
- **Termodinámica → SIA.** The one course that manipulates differentials constantly: in SIA
  $dU = T\,dS - P\,dV$ is legitimate as written, and Maxwell relations follow from exactness.
- Plus **Cálculo III → SIA** (microaffineness in several variables), **Álgebra Lineal II → GA**
  (a rotation's invariant object is a bivector *plane*, not an axis), **Física II → GA** (Kepler's
  second law and "angular momentum is conserved" are the same sentence, since $r\wedge p$ *is* area).

## 2. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** — 10 new twins (8 GA, 2 SIA) in `GA(8..15)` / `SIA(4..5)`, each in its own lens, each
  joined to its course by an `alternative formulation of` bridge in `BR(50..59)`.
- **AC2** — which lens twins which course is asserted **explicitly** in tests, not inferred from the
  title: the differential/integral spine and Termodinámica are SIA; vector calculus, algebra,
  mechanics, EM and QM are GA. (Cálculo IV is GA on purpose — SIA has nothing to say about the
  integral theorems, which is exactly what that course is.)
- **AC3** — `UNIVERSITAM_TWIN_PATH` extends to 20 steps in syllabus order, so the ⇄ walk is
  continuous from Introducción a la Física to Mecánica Cuántica I.
- **AC4** — every twin keeps the honest framing: the exam wants the official treatment, and each
  twin names its own limits (GA does not replace general linear algebra; SIA's mixed second
  derivatives need machinery the note does not develop).
- **AC5** — content renders in the app's markdown subset; additive; suite stays green.
